Kitchen Climate Control

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Salina HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. You get detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Salina HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Salina and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on rooftop and split systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we set up maintenance and monitoring so it doesn't happen again, and every response is fully documented for your records.

Preventive Care

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

Every Salina HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep before peak demand.

Fewer breakdowns, longer-lasting equipment, and lower energy costs: that's what our commercial clients consistently report after starting a program.
